## 4. Reviews

A separate agent reviewed the v2.0.4 diff three times (the first pass stopped on the usage limit and was resumed after
the reset). Pass 1: HIGH a service stopped before `startForeground` when a job ends at once (offline backups at start),
a shared link crashing Restore projects before storage access; MEDIUM a same-version backup deleting the release's APK,
two restores picking one project id, Switch to Private undone by a running Publish; LOW Private reported when nothing
changed, a Cancel lost before a request, Retry keeping screens and showing for non-retryable failures, a shared remark
across lanes, one list's rows used by another ListView, release files saved under a number. Pass 2: a stopping service
instance wiping the next start's flags, a stale idle() from the other lane, a restore waiting on the serial AsyncTask
queue. Pass 3: two calls made after releasing the lock. All fixed (commits bc11f9d, 87206ff, 208a80a); the renders found
the empty pill labels and the cut "Open on GitHub".
