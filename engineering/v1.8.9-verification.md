## 5. Size: why no R8

Dex is 45 MB of the 138 MB APK (110 MB uncompressed, a debug build); 20.5 MB of it is the embedded Kotlin compiler
(`org.jetbrains.kotlin`, Kotlin builds of projects), which finds classes through reflection and extension-point XML. Shrinking
it can't be checked here beyond one Kotlin fixture, so the IDE is not shrunk. Other savings (release-mode dex, x86 ABIs,
recompressed assets) add up to about 25 MB: still over 100 MB. The APK goes to the `binaries` branch as one file with Git
LFS (`apk/Sketchware_AI_v1.8.9.apk`).
